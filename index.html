02 · Technology & startup

Heart System

A hybrid ecosystem for personal transformation.

A digital and offline system where you become the main character. Safely process your fears, beliefs, and traumas through a unique blend of theatre and gaming.

01

PC & VR Games

Mass-market digital product

Interactive games with unique storylines, graphics, and choices. In key moments, the game asks you to perform physical actions, answer questions, or practice breathing techniques to safely work through common psychological challenges like stage fright or imposter syndrome.

02

Immersive Sessions

Premium offline & online product

  • Personal sessions: deeply customized 1-on-1 experiences with professional actors and psychologists.
  • Group sessions: theatrical experiences designed for team building, corporate groups, and school classes.
03

The Mirror Effect

The core mechanism

The core mechanism of the system. When you see a character in theater or in a game who acts out your fears, you recognize yourself. This recognition removes the feeling of loneliness and creates a safe space for change.

04

Science & Technology

Neurobiology · Psychology · AI

Backed by neurobiology and psychology. The system uses sound, visual effects, and physical practices as part of the gameplay. AI analysis processes your answers to provide detailed feedback and suggest deeper personal sessions, all under the supervision of professional psychologists.
